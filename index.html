About me

I'm enthusiastic about modeling, simulation, and control of dynamic mechanical systems, with interests in topics such as: GNC, mission analysis, multibody dynamics. I aim to deepen my expertise in numerical computing, software development and optimization. Currently I'm pursuing Master's degree in Space Engineering at Politecnico di Milano.

And a bit more

My knowledge is relatively broad, ranging from mechanical design and structural analysis to simulation and robotic software development. This is due to the fact that I was always interested in various types of moving mechanical systems. After joining a student team developing an electric motorcycle, I gained extensive knowledge of mechanical design and the multidisciplinary skills needed in motorsport. Over 3 years of contribution culminated with a succesful delivery of the motorcycle in a MotoStudent competition in Aragon, Spain. Besides that, I led a 3D pringitng association, conducted internships in automation software engineeering and structural analysis. In the end, what connected all my experiences was the design, simulation and control of various mechancal systems. Also, through code development since it gives freedom, unlike software packages for CAD or FEM analysis.
After graduating with a Bachelor's degree in Robotics, I decided to continue my Master's studies in Space Engineering to explore an industry focused on R&D and scientific innovation. My aim is to develop skills in software development for simulation and control of systems where multiple dynamical phenomena interfere.

In my free time I enjoy bouldering and cycling. I try to spend my vacations actively backpacking/ bikepacking. I hiked through Corsica on GR20 trail, completed Tour du Mont Blanc, Walkers Haute Route and cycled 1000km from Wien to Trieste
