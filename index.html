Short bio

I'm enthusiastic about modeling, simulation, and control of dynamic mechanical systems, with interests in topics such as: GNC, mission analysis, multibody dynamics. I aim to deepen my expertise in numerical computing, software development and optimization.

And a bit more

My knowledge is relatively broad, ranging from mechanical design and structural analysis to simulation and robotic software development. This is due to the fact that I was always interested in various types of moving mechanical systems. After joining a student team developing an electric motorcycle, I gained solid knowledge of mechanical design and the multidisciplinary knowledge needed in motorsport. This experience increased my curiosity about simulation engineering. In the end, I found FEM software extremely restrictive, but multibody dynamics truly interesting. After graduating with a Bachelor's degree in Robotics, I decided to continue my Master's studies in Space Engineering to explore an industry focused on R&D and scientific innovation. My aim is to develop skills in software development for simulation and control of systems where multiple dynamical phenomena interfere.

In my free time I enjoy bouldering and cycling. I try to spend my vacations actively backpacking/ bikepacking. I hiked through Corsica on GR20 trail, completed Tour du Mont Blanc, Walkers Haute Route and cycled 1000km from Wien to Trieste
