About me

I'm enthusiastic about modeling, simulation, and control of dynamic mechanical systems, with interests in topics such as: GNC, mission analysis, multibody dynamics. I aim to deepen my expertise in numerical computing, software development and optimization. Currently I'm pursuing Master's degree in Space Engineering at Politecnico di Milano.

My knowledge is relatively broad, ranging from mechanical design and structural analysis to simulation and robotic software development. This is due to the fact that I have always been interested in various types of moving mechanical systems. After joining a student team developing an electric motorcycle, I gained extensive knowledge of mechanical design and the multidisciplinary skills needed in motorsport. Over 3 years of contribution, this culminated in the successful delivery of the motorcycle at the MotoStudent competition in Aragon, Spain. Besides that, I led a 3D printing association, conducted internships in automation software engineering, and structural analysis. In the end, what connected all my experiences was the design, simulation, and control of various mechanical systems, as well as software development.
After graduating with a Bachelor's degree in Robotics, I decided to continue my Master's studies in Space Engineering to explore an industry focused on R&D and scientific innovation. My aim is to develop skills in software development for the simulation and control of systems where multiple dynamical phenomena interact.

In my free time, I enjoy bouldering and cycling. I try to spend my vacations actively backpacking or bikepacking. I have hiked through Corsica on the GR20 trail, completed the Tour du Mont Blanc, Walkers Haute Route, and cycled 1,000 km from Vienna to Trieste.
